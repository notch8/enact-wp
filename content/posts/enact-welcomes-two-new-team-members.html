Dr Adam Vials Moore, who joins Enact as Repository Manager, advocates for the outputs of research to be openly available and easy to discover and access. Adam has experience across a wide array of enabling technologies and infrastructures, including metacognitive and adaptive learning, hypertext, bioinformatics and RIM/repository architecture. His current interests focus on ensuring that the global connectivity enabled by PIDs, infrastructure and metadata allows for equitable discovery and access for all scholarly work, and developing the intertwingular nature of hyperconnected information structures.

Dr Victoria Burgher is Enact's new Project Co-ordinator. She is a mixed-media artist, researcher and educator whose work spans interdisciplinary creative practice and collaborative social justice projects. She completed her practice-based PhD at the Centre for Research in Education, Arts and Media (CREAM), University of Westminster, in December 2025.
