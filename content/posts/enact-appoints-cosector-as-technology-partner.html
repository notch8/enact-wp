We are very excited to announce that following a tender process CoSector: University of London has been appointed as the Enact Technology Partner.

Working closely with the Enact Project Team and digital repository developer Notch8, CoSector brings extensive experience of collaborating with practice research-led institutions and applying the co-design approach that is central to Enact.

Using the open-source Samvera Hyku platform, supported by a strong global community, provides the flexibility needed to showcase practice research narratives, processes and outputs. It also enables Enact's project-centred approach, supports the implementation of the PR Voices practice research metadata schema – developed through the PR Voices and SPARKLE projects – prioritises an excellent user experience and ensures integration with global open information standards.
