About the project

The Enact: PR Voices and SPARKLE Phase 2 project has received UKRI Digital Research Infrastructure (DRI) funding through AHRC's Infrastructure for Digital Arts and Humanities (iDAH) programme. Grant number AH/Z000769/1

Our vision

Enact's vision is to enable practice researchers to share the full narrative of their creations. This includes the methods, processes and context underpinning them, and to enhance the reach, persistence, connectability and recognition of the value of their creations in the wider world.

More about Enact's vision and mission

Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable practice research

Beyond format

Capturing and preserving the context and narrative of process and outputs, and making process and output content, connections and narrative visible.

Beyond author

Recognising the range of contributions to the creation of all forms of knowledge, from creators and collaborators to participants.

Ethics and rights

Respecting ethics and intellectual property in how practice research is shared, evidenced and reused.

Building the Enact repository

Following a tender process, CoSector: University of London has been appointed as the Enact Technology Partner, working closely with the Enact Project Team and digital repository developer Notch8. The repository is built on the open-source Samvera Hyku platform, which provides the flexibility needed to showcase practice research narratives, processes and outputs, and supports the PR Voices practice research metadata schema.

The project at a glance

Year 1: Establishment (2025-26)

Project blog launch, Project Board and Community Advisory Group set up. Technology Partner appointed and the Enact Core repository build begins, including participation from Early Adopter Pathfinder projects.

Year 2: Launch and consolidation (2026-27)

Enact Core Repository Minimum Viable Product launch, Enact Pathfinder Challengers join, and the skills and training pilot programme is designed and delivered.

Year 3: Expansion (2027-28)

Additional Pathfinder projects onboarded and an updated skills and training programme delivered.
