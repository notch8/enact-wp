Enact: PR Voices and SPARKLE Phase 2 is a three-year programme to build a practice research repository and the community, skills and standards around it.

At a glance

Year 1: Establishment (2025-26)

Project blog launch, Project Board and Community Advisory Group set up, Technology partner appointed and Enact Core repository build begins, including participation from Early Adopter Pathfinder projects.

Year 2: Launch and consolidation (2026-27)

Enact Core Repository Minimum Viable Product (Enact: Core) launch, Enact Pathfinder Challengers join and skills and training pilot programme delivered.

Year 3: Expansion (2027-28)

Additional Pathfinders onboarded and updated skills and training programme delivered.
