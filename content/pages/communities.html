Enact's communities include discipline-based academics and researchers and those with specialist skills to enable sharing of practice research, from repository and research data managers to curators, research managers, software developers and more. Each community will have a different interest in and/or way of engaging with Enact.

Enact will also engage with a wide range of communities that have an interest in Enact, which include discipline communities (e.g. the Royal Musical Association, the Council for Higher Education in Art and Design (CHEAD)), persistent identifier communities (e.g. the UK ORCID Community, DataCite and Research Activity Identifier (RAiD)), research data communities (Research Data Alliance, Digital Curation Centre, Digital Preservation Coalition) and more.

To hear about opportunities to get involved, join the mailing list or contact the team.
